What is NER?¶
  1. Name Entity Recognition is a techinque used in NLP that recognizes the names in a text, and and classifies them to their corresponding categories.
  2. There are key categoories that a noun is placed in each such as a person, place, and etc.
  3. There is a BIO notation that differntiates between the beginning (B), inside (I), and outside (O), of a scharacter to make it easy to recognize a named entity. 0 also means non-entity tokens

Steps in NER:¶

  1. Injesting or loading the datasheet
  2. Extracting or detecting the entitites from the text
  3. Classifying them into their corresponding categories

Applications in NER:¶

  1. Information retreival
  2. Content recommendation
  3. In data analysis, it is extracting key patterns and trends

Categories in NER:¶

Typical Categories in NRE are:

  1. PER: person
  2. ORG: organization
  3. LOC: location
  4. GPE: geopolitical entity
  5. DATE: date
  6. MONEY: monetary values

  1. NRE breaks words into categories such as (people, org, loc, geopolitical entity, date, or monetary value).

  3. The df. dataframe allows you to display the data stored in the entities and they can be tranferred to the pandas dataframe.

  6. Entites can only be displayed after spaCy and displacy are deployed. They are deployed using the pandas dataframe.
  7. The data can be converted into an NLP document using doc = nlp(text).
