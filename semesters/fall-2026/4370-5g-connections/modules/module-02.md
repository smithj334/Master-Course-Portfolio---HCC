# Module 2 — Network Architecture and Wireless Basics

## What this module is about

How networks are put together — the pieces between a device and the internet — and how wireless links behave. Distance, interference, and the way a signal weakens as it travels all shape what a network can actually deliver.

## What I took away

A network is a chain of pieces, and a weak link anywhere slows the whole thing down. That idea came back in the midterm routing simulation, where packets queued up behind the slowest router and the whole trip got longer because of it.
