# Module 11 — 6G Vision and AI-Native Networks

## What this module is about

What comes after 5G: higher frequencies, new kinds of services, and AI built into the network from the start instead of bolted on afterward. Digital twins of networks — a live model of the network you can test changes on before you make them — are part of that vision.
