# Module 1 — Telecommunications Fundamentals

## What this module is about

How information travels. A call, a video, a text — it is all a signal carrying information, and the way that signal is built decides how well it survives the trip. This module is the base layer: signals, modulation, and what a network actually moves. Everything later in the course builds on it.

## What I took away

Before this module, a phone call or a video loading was just something that happened. Now I know there is a signal carrying it, and the quality of that signal is the first thing that decides how well the network performs.
