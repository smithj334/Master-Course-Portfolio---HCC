# Module 9 — Sustainable AI and Green Communications

## What this module is about

Networks and AI use a lot of power. This module is about running them with less waste — more efficient hardware, smarter use of energy, and AI that helps the network use only what it needs.

## What I worked on

- Coursework notebooks for this module are in [notebooks/](../notebooks/).

## What I took away

Efficiency is not a side issue. The cheapest watt is the one you don't use, and AI can help find it — by predicting demand and powering down what is not needed.
