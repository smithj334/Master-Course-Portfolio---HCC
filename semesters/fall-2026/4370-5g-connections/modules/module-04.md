# Module 4 — 5G Network Architecture and AI Integration

## What this module is about

What makes 5G different: faster speeds, lower delay, and a network built to carry many kinds of traffic at once — with AI helping manage it. The architecture is designed so different services can share the same network without getting in each other's way.

## What I worked on

- **Midterm Project, Part 1** — SimPy simulation of 100 packets through three routers in a chain. 100 delivered, average latency 5.39, fastest 2.80, slowest 11.35, average time waiting in line 2.59. See [../projects/midterm/](../projects/midterm/).
- Coursework notebooks for this module are in [notebooks/](../notebooks/).

## What I took away

Speed on paper is not speed in practice. Waiting in line behind a busy router is what users actually feel — and that is the problem AI in a 5G network is trying to solve.
