# Module 8 — Cybersecurity and AI in Telecommunications

## What this module is about

How networks get attacked, and how AI helps spot attacks — including the risk that AI itself can be fooled. Security is not a feature added at the end; it has to be part of how the network is designed and run.

## What I worked on

- Coursework notebooks for this module are in [notebooks/](../notebooks/).

## What I took away

A network that cannot defend itself is not a finished network, no matter how fast it is. Speed means nothing if the traffic on it cannot be trusted.
