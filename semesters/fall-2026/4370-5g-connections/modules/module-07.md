# Module 7 — IoT and Edge Computing with AI

## What this module is about

Billions of small devices sending data — sensors, machines, vehicles — and why some of the thinking has to happen close to the device, at the edge, instead of far away in a data center. When a decision has to be fast, the round trip to the cloud is too slow.

## What I worked on

- Coursework notebooks for this module are in [notebooks/](../notebooks/).

## What I took away

Not every decision can wait for a round trip to the cloud. Some have to be made where the data is, and that is what edge computing is for.
