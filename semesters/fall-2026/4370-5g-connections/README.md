## Selected project highlights

- **Midterm Projects (submitted Oct 29):** SimPy routing simulation, Mesa agent-based modeling, and a scikit-learn model that predicts latency and picks the faster route. See [projects/midterm/](projects/midterm/).
- **SimPy routing:** 100 packets through three routers — 100 delivered, average latency 5.39, fastest 2.80, slowest 11.35, average time waiting in line 2.59.
- **Mesa agents:** users move off a congested router on their own — 6 reroutes in the first step, then almost none, ending split 4 / 3 / 3 across three routers.
- **scikit-learn routing:** a Random Forest trained on 500 simulated traffic measurements predicts latency, then routes by prediction — stay put at low load, reroute at high load even with a 15 ms detour.
