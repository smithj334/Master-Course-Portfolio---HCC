## Selected highlights

- **Midterm simulations (ITAI 4370, Fall 2026):** SimPy routing simulation, Mesa agent-based modeling, and a scikit-learn model that predicts latency and picks the faster route. 100 packets through three routers — 100 delivered, average latency 5.39. See [semesters/fall-2026/4370-5g-connections/projects/midterm/](semesters/fall-2026/4370-5g-connections/projects/midterm/).
- **Deep Learning final (ITAI 2376, Fall 2025):** CNNs and diffusion models, including a diffusion-models notebook and a final project.
- **AI Applications final (ITAI 2372, Spring 2026):** case studies across healthcare, agriculture, fraud detection, entertainment, blockchain, retail, and manufacturing, plus an AI-based stock news sentiment tracker.
- **Digital Twins (ITAI 4371, Fall 2026):** a 3D-printed micro-home and a wall panel built in Blender and exported as OpenUSD — Lab 2 and Lab 3 capstone assets. The Blender files and the .usda export are binary, listed in [BINARY-INVENTORY.md](BINARY-INVENTORY.md).
- **SeniorSite AI (Data Curation, Fall 2025):** the project plan for an AI senior-housing site selection system, built out as the [SeniorSite-AI](https://github.com/smithj334/SeniorSite-AI) repo.
- **Scout AI trade advisor (AI Business & Finance, Spring 2026):** the tech-stack plan for a Replit-based AI trade advisor app.
- **Computer Vision (ITAI 1378, Fall 2024):** a CIFAR-10 classical machine learning image classifier, plus Lab 5 and the group midterm.
- **NLP labs (Fall 2024):** labs 01 through 09 — NLTK toolkit work with exported HTML versions of the labs.

## Evidence of growth

**Python basics → machine learning, computer vision, NLP → cybersecurity and data → deep learning, cloud, data curation → AI applications, robotics, finance → 5G/AI-native networks, digital twins, AI policy.**

I started with short Python exercises and a history-of-AI team project. Two years later I was simulating network traffic, training models to predict latency, building 3D capstone assets in Blender, and writing about AI governance. The through-line is building things: every course that stuck with me is one where I made something that ran.
